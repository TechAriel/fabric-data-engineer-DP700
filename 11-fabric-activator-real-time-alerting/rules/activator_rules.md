# Activator Rules

## Rule: Medicine temp out of range

### Condition

Temperature > 20°C

---

### Filters

City = Redmond  
SpecialCare = Medicine  
ColdChainType = Refrigerated  

---

### Action

Send Email Alert

---

### Purpose

Ensure temperature-sensitive medical shipments remain within safe limits during transit.

---

### Business Impact

- Prevents spoilage of medical products
- Enables proactive intervention
- Improves delivery reliability
